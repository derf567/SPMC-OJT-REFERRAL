🎵 REQUIRED FILES:
   
   ✓ chanak.mp3          ← Your custom chanak sound
   ✓ default.mp3         ← Default notification sound
   ✓ bell.mp3            ← Bell sound
   ✓ chime.mp3           ← Chime sound
   ✓ ding.mp3            ← Ding sound
   ✓ beep.mp3            ← Beep sound

📝 OPTIONAL FILES (for urgent notifications):
   
   ○ chanak-urgent.mp3   ← Urgent version of chanak
   ○ bell-urgent.mp3     ← Urgent version of bell
   ○ etc.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

📍 CURRENT LOCATION:
   SPMC/front-end/public/notification-sounds/

🎯 QUICK START:
   1. Copy your chanak.mp3 file to this folder
   2. Refresh your browser
   3. Click the speaker icon (🔊) in the app
   4. Click the music note (🎵) to select sound
   5. Choose "Chanak" and test it!

💡 TIP: Keep sound files under 100KB and 1-3 seconds long
