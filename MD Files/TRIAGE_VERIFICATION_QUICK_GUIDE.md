# Triage Verification Workflow - Quick Guide

## For Triage/EDCC Personnel

### What Changed?
Previously, when all departments accepted a referral, the referrer was automatically notified to fill the transit form. Now, you (triage/EDCC) must verify the acceptance with the departments before the referrer is notified.

### Step-by-Step Process

#### 1. Monitor Referrals
- Go to **Triage Referrals** page
- Look for referrals with status **"Awaiting Verification"** (orange badge)
- These are referrals where departments have accepted and are waiting for your verification

#### 2. View Department Acceptances
- Click **"View Status"** button on the referral
- A dialog will show:
  - Which departments accepted
  - Who accepted and when
  - Total acceptance count vs. needed count
  - Any notes from departments

#### 3. Call the Department
- Use the department contact information to call them
- Verify the acceptance and discuss any details
- Confirm they are ready to receive the patient

#### 4. Approve for Transit
- After verifying with the department, click **"Approve for Transit"** button
- A dialog will appear showing:
  - Referral details
  - Department acceptance summary
  - List of accepting departments
  - Optional verification notes field

#### 5. Add Verification Notes (Optional)
- In the "Verification Notes" field, you can add:
  - Confirmation of department readiness
  - Any special instructions discussed
  - Time of verification call
  - Any other relevant information

#### 6. Confirm Approval
- Click **"Approve for Transit"** button
- The referral status will change to "Dispositioned"
- The referrer will be notified to fill the transit form

### Status Progression
```
Waiting Acceptance (Departments deciding)
    ↓
Awaiting Verification (Departments accepted, waiting for your verification)
    ↓
Dispositioned (You approved, referrer notified)
    ↓
In Transit (Referrer filled transit form)
```

### Key Points
- ✅ Only triage/EDCC personnel can approve referrals for transit
- ✅ You must verify with departments before approving
- ✅ Verification notes are recorded for audit trail
- ✅ Referrer is only notified after you approve
- ✅ All actions are tracked in the referral history

### Troubleshooting

**Q: I don't see the "Approve for Transit" button**
- A: The referral must be in "Awaiting Verification" status. Check the status badge color (should be orange).

**Q: Can I add notes after approving?**
- A: No, notes must be added before clicking "Approve for Transit". You can add them in the verification notes field.

**Q: What if the department is not ready?**
- A: You can click "View Status" to see the details, then use "Redirect" to reassign to different departments if needed.

**Q: How do I know when a referral is ready for verification?**
- A: The system will show referrals with "Awaiting Verification" status in the Triage Referrals page. You can also filter by this status.
